# Data License and Usage

This data package is published for open use.

Important: the dataset combines material from multiple upstream sources.  
Your rights and obligations come from those upstream licenses and terms.

## Practical Summary

1. You may use this package for personal, academic, and commercial projects.
2. You must keep attribution to required sources.
3. For OpenStreetMap-derived data, ODbL requirements apply (including attribution and share-alike obligations for derivative databases where applicable).
4. Some sources have their own terms of use that must be followed.
